01 A note before
the numbers

A little while ago one of your LinkedIn posts came across my feed. I wasn’t looking for work — I just kept reading. The thinking was clear, generous and unusually honest about how women actually build businesses. So I did the thing good writing makes you do: I clicked through to your profile to find out more.

And there the trail stopped. A strong voice, a real methodology, clients who clearly valued the work — but no home of your own on the web. Everything pointed to a studio that didn’t yet have a front door. That gap is exactly the kind of problem I like, so rather than send a pitch, I built the answer first.

The more I read, the more the site wanted a specific shape. Your work moves people from competing advice and noise, through the signal of what is actually true for them, into a decision, and then into sustainable movement. That isn’t a template. So I designed around it — a Red Thread framework, built on your own Align → Strategise → Act method as its spine — and made the site behave like the coaching it represents.

I built the website first, then earned the right to send the invoice second. You are holding the invoice — the website is already yours to see.
On why this document exists at all

So this statement has one job: to make the price make sense. Custom work like this usually arrives as a single intimidating number with no explanation. I’d rather show you the seams — every part of the work, what a studio would ordinarily charge for it, and then the deliberately small figure I’m asking, because this began as a gesture, not a sale. No pressure sits behind any of it. If it isn’t right for you, that is a completely fine answer.

The pages that follow: 02 — what was actually built, in plain terms. 03 — the cost, split line by line, with the reason for each. 04 — friendly terms, and how little is due to begin.
